## What cannot be checked automatically

These checks prove the PDF is drawn at the exact size. What comes out of the printer also depends on the print dialog (scaling) and the printer's paper feed. Before printing the full set, print page 1 and measure the test card (56 × 87 mm inside the line) and the 200 mm ruler with a real ruler. The PDF asks viewers not to scale (`PrintScaling None`), but not every viewer or driver honours it.
