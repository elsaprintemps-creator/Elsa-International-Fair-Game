Review and correct the English and French text of every card. Gates A–D have a question and a matching answer card; Gate E has open discussion questions only.

How saving works
  1. Edit any text box. A live preview shows the printed card and warns you if the text is too long.
  2. Your edits are saved automatically in this browser, on this computer only. They are not sent anywhere, other people cannot see them, and clearing your browser data deletes them.
  3. When you are done, click Export JSON and send the downloaded file to the project coordinator. Export CSV gives the same content for Excel; Change list lists only what you changed.
  4. To continue on another computer, or to review someone else's file, use Import JSON.

This page cannot change the project files directly: the coordinator replaces data/cards.json with your export and rebuilds the PDFs.
